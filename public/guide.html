The full tour

What each part of your dashboard does

In the order they appear in your menu.

📊 Analytics

How many people have enquired recently, and how many products and categories you have. A simple day-by-day chart of enquiries.

🎨 Website

Pick which of your three designs is active, choose which sections show (Products, Gallery, Offers, and so on), and set what your two main buttons do — call, WhatsApp, jump to a section, or a link of your choice.

🏬 Business Details

Your name, phone, WhatsApp, hours, address, Google Maps link, Instagram — everything that appears on your site's contact info.

🗂️ Categories

Group your products — "Cold Pressed Oils", "Sarees Under ₹1,500", whatever makes sense for your shop. Reorder or hide any of them any time.

📦 Products

Add products one at a time, or all at once from a spreadsheet (Upload in Bulk). Each one can have a photo, price, an offer price, and a stock count.

📖 Catalogue

A shareable page listing your products by category — pick which categories appear and whether prices show. Updates itself as your products change.

🖼️ Gallery

Upload photos of your shop, your products in use, events — whatever helps customers picture the place.

🏷️ Offers

A limited-time deal with a start and end date. It appears and disappears from your site automatically — you don't have to remember to take it down.

💸 Discounts

An always-on discount (like "10% off, code SAVE10") shown as a small banner on your site — no date range needed.

📣 Campaigns

Write a message once, then send it over WhatsApp to anyone who's enquired with you before — one tap per person.

💬 Messages

Every enquiry submitted through your site lands here, with one-tap Call and WhatsApp buttons to reply.

🔍 SEO

Helps people searching Google find you. Leave it blank and use "Suggest with AI" for a starting draft — always worth a quick read before saving.

Going live

How publishing works

Two separate things are always true: what you've saved, and what's actually live for visitors. Publishing is what moves one to the other.

Edit anything, any time

Changes are saved as you go, but visitors to your site won't see them yet.

Hit Publish (in Settings)

This makes everything you've changed go live at once. You'll see how many changes are waiting before you publish.

Check History any time

Every publish is saved as a version — useful if you ever want to see what changed and when.

🔒 If Settings shows publishing as locked, that just means we need to switch it on for your account — message us and we'll turn it on.

Common questions

Questions people ask

I made changes but my website still looks the same

Changes are saved as drafts first — go to Settings and hit Publish to make them visible to visitors.

Why can't I click Publish?

Publishing may need to be switched on for your account first — reach out to us at 📞 Call💬 WhatsApp and we'll enable it.

How do I add a photo to a product?

Go to Products, open the product (or add a new one), and use the image upload field. JPG, PNG or WEBP files under 5MB work best.
